- Developing the React Native app and shipping new building-management features.
- Migrated the backend from .NET to Go and split it into microservices.
- Integrated an AI chatbot for reservations and user acquisition.
- Set up notifications end to end: Novu for orchestration, Resend for email and Expo for push to the native app.
- Track product events with PostHog.
- Self-host every service on our own VPS with Docker and Traefik, including domains, routing, S3-compatible storage, backups and CI/CD.
